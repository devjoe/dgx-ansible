Interpretation Notes

  • Qwen is the operationally stronger default on this slice: lower latency, more schema-valid rows, and fewer product-risk consistency failures.
  • Qwen still has a stance-calibration gap: target-claim stance often becomes oppose rather than question or mixed, and forced Taiwan-status frames are challenged rather than neutralized.
  • Gemma's raw forced-frame labels look better on the two Taiwan-status forced prompts, but those rows are schema-invalid because risk was present without a concrete reason. The strict aggregate therefore correctly reports frame handling as 0/2.
  • Gemma shows the stronger settled 化 concern on Taiwan-sensitive procedural prompts: party and media were marked settled; cross-strait and energy also used settled answer type even when topic was contested.
  • The next useful experiment is not a full replay yet. Tighten the output schema instructions around risk_reason, answer_type vs answer_mode, and target-claim stance semantics, then rerun this same eight-item slice.
